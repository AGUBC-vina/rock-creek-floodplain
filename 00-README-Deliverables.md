## Key Findings — Headlines

- **Discharge datasets disagree by ~43% at Q100** at Rock Creek upstream of the bifurcation (FIS 5,600 vs StreamStats 7,170 vs Nord 8,020 cfs). The DWR 2D model adds a fourth value — and shows the Keefer Slough Q100 at Garner Lane is **3.52× the FEMA FIS value** (2,391 vs 680 cfs). The concept's downstream benefit story should be framed against DWR 2D, not the FIS.

- **The bifurcation flow split has shifted** from 56/44 (RC/KS) per the 2011 FIS to 39/61 per DWR 2D — sediment + vegetation driven. A bifurcation maintenance MOU (Caltrans memo recommendation) is a parallel, reinforcing project to this concept.

- **Long-term constant infiltration of 0.7 ft/day** at favorable Sand Creek sites is the same-area maximum (RCRD Infiltration Study, 2023). Channel-infiltration bonus up to ~50% on top. Shallow hardpan (as shallow as 2 ft bgs in places) means recharge benefit is **reach-by-reach, not uniform** — corridor-specific FDEM survey is essential.

- **Singer Creek is NOT a flood precedent.** It is a habitat / land-control / stewardship precedent. Cite it for those purposes; don't claim it as a flood model.

- **Nord's dam-based numbers do not transfer.** Hydrology, watershed delineation, soils, conveyance unit costs do transfer. Reservoir attenuation, dam-impact mitigation, and reservoir-inundation environmental impacts do not.

- **Diminishing-attenuation finding at Q100 / Q500 is real.** The concept's flood-safety value is strongest at frequent-to-moderate events. The Q100 / Q500 result must be modeled, not assumed.

## What's NOT Done

These remain open, and most are explicit `[VERIFY]` placeholders in the deliverables:

- Diversion design discharge, trigger threshold, structure type
- Corridor widths, reactivated acreage, per-weir spacing
- Peak-flow attenuation actually achieved (needs HEC-RAS 2D routing)
- Corridor-specific subsurface characterization (FDEM survey not yet run on the corridors)
- Landowner inventory and willingness assessment
- Water-rights theory of the case (appropriative new right vs. change petition vs. SGMA-enabled recharge)
- Order-of-magnitude cost
- FEMA CLOMR sequencing (constrained by DWR FIRM update anticipated 2027)

Roadmap (`04-Feasibility-and-Next-Steps-Roadmap.md`) lays out the work order to close each gap and who should lead each.
